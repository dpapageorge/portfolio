Danny Papageorge Process spec · From stuck to shipped · Rev. 01
1

Scope It

Stage 1 · The real problem

A working session to pin down what you're building, who it's for, and what done looks like.

Deliverables

  • Kickoff call & the hard questions
  • Audit of what exists today
  • Goals, audiences & constraints
  • A definition of "done"
  • An honest fit check
Output A one-page brief we both believe.
2

Explore

Stage 2 · Directions

Multiple directions as working prototypes, not mood boards.

AI-assisted tooling means more ground covered in days, not weeks. You react to something real.

Deliverables

  • 3+ creative directions
  • Prototypes you can click
  • Reference & inspiration boards
  • Alignment on one direction
Output One direction, chosen with evidence.
3

Design

Stage 3 · The build-out

The chosen direction, developed end to end.

Deliverables

  • Flows & UX
  • Interface design
  • Design system or identity system
  • Type, color & illustration
  • Prototypes for testing
Output A locked design, and the system behind it.
4

Ship

Stage 4 · Launch

Design that survives contact with engineering.

Deliverables

  • Engineering handoff & specs
  • Build support & QA passes
  • Production art & assets
  • Launch materials
  • The occasional pep talk
Output The thing, live, in people's hands.
5

Keep It Sharp

Stage 5 · Support

Launch day is the start, not the finish line.

Deliverables

  • Post-launch tune-ups
  • Analytics & iteration
  • New features, pages or drops
  • Ongoing support, if you want it
  • All working files, handed over
Output A product that keeps getting better after launch.
